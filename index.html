About

An experiment: an English and Dagaare lookup for words and example sentences. Central Dagaare only, The words and sentences come from A Dictionary and Grammatical Sketch of Dagaare (2021) by Mark Ali, Scott Grimm and Adams Bodomo, published by Language Science Press under a CC BY 4.0 license. They were cleaned and reformatted for this app. Entries are unverified. English audio uses your device’s voice. Dagaare audio plays only when audio files are placed next to this page, otherwise it says there is none yet. Camera and voice input are not built yet.
